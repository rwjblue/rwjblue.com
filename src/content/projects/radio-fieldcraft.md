Radio work belongs here as a technical practice, not a side category. Portable
HF, CW, station setup, logging, antennas, and field failures all produce notes
worth keeping.

## On the bench

The current build queue crosses antennas, firmware, and small-scale hardware:

- Two [Pacific Antenna dual-band trap dipoles](/radio/equipment/pacific-antenna-dual-band-trap-dipoles/),
  covering 20/40 and 17/30 meters;
- a [homebrew six-band linked dipole](/radio/equipment/homebrew-six-band-linked-dipole/)
  for 15 through 80 meters, beginning with a simpler feedpoint prototype; and
- a [Quansheng UV-K1](/radio/equipment/quansheng-uv-k1/) prepared for the NR7Y
  CW firmware and external-paddle support.

The equipment pages are the stable descriptions of what is being built. Dated
notes can record the assembly process, measurements, mistakes, and field results
without turning the inventory into a build diary. Once the antenna work is
complete, the trapped and linked dipoles can also be compared as portable
systems: packed size, deployment time, tuning, bandwidth, and what actually
gets carried into the field.
