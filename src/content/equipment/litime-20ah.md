The 20 Ah LiTime fills the space between the small Bioenno packs and the
car-oriented 50 Ah battery. It has enough reserve for longer portable sessions
while remaining much more practical to move than the 280 Ah shack battery.

The exact LiTime sub-variant is not asserted here because the current
manufacturer catalog includes multiple 20 Ah configurations. Capacity, brand,
chemistry, and field role are the confirmed facts.
