The Reliance Rybakov packages a 4:1 transformer, 25-foot radiating wire, and four
17-foot radials into a small portable antenna. It fills the multiband wire
vertical role when a telescoping whip would be awkward or unnecessary.

It is distinct from the Rybakov configurations assembled from the
[REZ Scout-XF modules](/radio/equipment/rez-scout-xf/), even though both use the
same broad antenna idea.
