The KX3 is a flexible portable station with an internal tuner and enough
controls on the front panel to support experimentation without depending on a
computer.

It has been paired with the REZ Scout system, wire antennas, and compact CW
paddles for multi-stop outings such as the [May 2026 Rhode Island POTA rove](/notes/2026-05-25-pota-rove/)
and the [Flight of the Bumblebees operation from Block Island](/notes/2026-07-26-flight-of-the-bumblebees-from-block-island/).
