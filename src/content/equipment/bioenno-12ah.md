The 12 Ah battery sits between the tiny walking kit and the large vehicle power
system. It is portable enough for a picnic table while providing useful reserve
for higher-power radios or longer sessions.

Its behavior during the [first day of the Rhode Island-to-Florida rove](/notes/2026-06-19-rhode-island-to-florida-rove-day-one/)
also made it a useful reminder to test field batteries under load before a trip.
