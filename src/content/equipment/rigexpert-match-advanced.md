The MATCH provides measurements before a radio is connected, which is useful
for distinguishing antenna problems from tuner behavior or operating error. It
travels when an activation is also an antenna experiment.

It was used to investigate the long-wire setup during the [K2HRC Rhode Island POTA activation](/notes/2026-07-18-k2hrc-rhode-island-pota/).
