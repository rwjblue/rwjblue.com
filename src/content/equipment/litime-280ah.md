The 280 Ah LiTime is the energy reserve behind the home station. It feeds the
radio power path through the
[West Mountain Radio Epic PWRgate](/radio/equipment/west-mountain-radio-epic-pwrgate/),
which manages the relationship between the shack supply, battery, and connected
loads.

This is intentionally separate from the smaller portable batteries: it is
station infrastructure, not something packed for an ordinary activation.
