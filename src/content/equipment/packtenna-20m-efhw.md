The 20-meter PackTenna is the resonant counterpart to the 29-foot random wire.
It provides a small, single-wire option when the operating plan is centered on
20 meters and avoiding a wide-range tuner is useful.

The transformer, winder, and wire stay together as one deployable antenna. Coax,
throw line, and the support holding its far end remain reusable field-kit parts
rather than separate pieces of this entry.
