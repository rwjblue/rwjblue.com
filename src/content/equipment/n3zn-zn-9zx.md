The ZN-9ZX is the heavier, desk-oriented counterpart to the tiny field paddles.
Its stable base and deliberate feel are useful for longer CW sessions and for
portable operations where carrying weight is not the main constraint.

It has also traveled for car and picnic-table stations, including the
[Rhode Island-to-Florida rove](/notes/2026-06-22-rhode-island-to-florida-rove-day-four/).
