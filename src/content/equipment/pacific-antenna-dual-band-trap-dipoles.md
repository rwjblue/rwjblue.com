The pair includes Pacific Antenna's 20/40-meter and 17/30-meter dual-band
trap-dipole kits. Each is intended to become a lightweight resonant option for
portable operating, with the two antennas collectively covering four useful HF
bands.

Both kits still need to be assembled and tuned. The build will provide a chance
to compare their common construction, record final element dimensions and
analyzer measurements, and see how the compact trapped design behaves in actual
field deployments.
