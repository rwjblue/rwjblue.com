The 50 Ah battery is part of the car-adjacent station rather than the walking
kit. It supports the FT-891 and related equipment when operating time, transmit
power, or travel-day flexibility matters more than carried weight.

It served as the main power reserve during the [Rhode Island-to-Florida POTA rove](/notes/2026-06-19-rhode-island-to-florida-rove-day-one/).
