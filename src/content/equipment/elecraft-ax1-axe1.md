The AX1 is a loaded whip for 20, 17, and 15 meters; the AXE1 extender adds
40-meter operation. The set packs into very little space and can mount directly
to a radio, on the AXB1 bipod, or on a small tripod.

It is useful when a wire or full-size vertical will not fit, but the small
radiator is a deliberate compromise. The
[Washington DC rove](/notes/2026-06-21-washington-dc-pota-rove/) records both
its value at a crowded public site and the patience it required on a marginal
band.
