The CSP-1 is a compact single-lever paddle from W4ARB. It has a lovely, tight
action and is small enough for portable use without being too small to hold
comfortably. In normal paddle use with an electronic keyer, it already feels
excellent.

It can also be used as a sideswiper, or cootie. That style is new to me; my
sending experience has been almost entirely with paddles and electronic keyers,
so I will update this after more practice.
