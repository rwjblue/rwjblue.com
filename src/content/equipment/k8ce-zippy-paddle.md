The Zippy is a regular part of the portable CW kit. It is light enough to carry
without much thought but stable and predictable enough to rescue an activation
when another key is awkward or unreliable.

It has been used with both the KX2 and KX3, including the [May 2026 Rhode Island POTA rove](/notes/2026-05-25-pota-rove/)
and a [humid Florida hike-in activation](/notes/2026-06-25-ross-prairie-twofer-pota/).
