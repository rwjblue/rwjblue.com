The POTA20 is a compact support for activations where a full antenna system
would add unnecessary setup time. It is most useful with lightweight wire and a
nearby tie-off or tree carrying the far end.

It supported the feed point for the long wire used during the [K2HRC Rhode Island POTA activation](/notes/2026-07-18-k2hrc-rhode-island-pota/).
