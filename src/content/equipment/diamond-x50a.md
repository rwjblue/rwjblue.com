The X50A is installed in the attic and is the primary home antenna for
2-meter and 70-centimeter operation. It covers the local repeater, net, and
monitoring role that the portable HF antennas do not.

It is kept as a complete station antenna rather than split into separate
inventory entries for its feed line, mounting hardware, and adapters.
