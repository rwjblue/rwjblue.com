The inventory includes two assembled QMX radios: the 60/40/30/20/17/15-meter
mid-band version and the 20/17/15/12/11/10-meter high-band version. I do not
have an assembled low-band 80/60/40/30/20-meter QMX. A third QMX remains an
unbuilt kit; its intended band configuration still needs to be confirmed.

The QMX has a strong feature set for such a small radio, but it has not yet
become part of my regular field rotation. The Elecraft portable radios have
generally won on fit, finish, and familiarity. Deliberately rotating a QMX into
future outings would help me learn it well enough to decide where it belongs.
