The Pro 7 is the main full-size home-station headset. Its isolation and
comfortable ear pads are useful for longer operating sessions, while stereo
audio, balance, and phase controls help when listening for weak or crowded
signals.

It was the listening setup for my first
[CQ WPX CW learning weekend](/notes/2026-05-31-cq-wpx-cw-learning-weekend/),
paired with the K4D while I worked through fast contest exchanges.
