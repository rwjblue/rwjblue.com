The FT-891 fills the higher-power portable role. It is small enough for a
vehicle or picnic-table station while providing more transmit power for short
operating windows, SSB, or difficult conditions.

It became the main radio for the [Rhode Island-to-Florida POTA rove](/notes/2026-06-19-rhode-island-to-florida-rove-day-one/)
and has also worked well with longer field wires and larger batteries.
