The PERformer is Greg Mihran, KJ6ER's portable 40-through-6-meter resonant
quarter-wave vertical design. It uses a telescoping whip and tuned elevated
radials, with the radiator and radial lengths changed for the selected band.
Mine is the
[complete system assembled by J-Systems](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4399509317/performer-7-band-quarterwave-antenna),
whose build quality has been excellent.

[KJ6ER's antenna resource page](https://www.k6ldf.com/greg-mihran-kj6er/)
includes the antenna primer and the complete
[PERformer plans and specifications](https://bit.ly/KJ6ERPERformer).
The design is published for anyone who wants to build it; I chose the assembled
J-Systems version because I did not have time to make one myself.

I have used it with the REZ 17-foot whip and loading coil plus the 40-meter
extension radials. The
[East Bay rove](/notes/2026-05-30-east-bay-pota-rove/) records that setup at
Eight Rod Farm, including the loose coupler that initially made the SWR look
wrong.
