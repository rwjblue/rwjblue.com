The three 3 Ah Bioenno batteries are the light external-power options in the
portable kit. One can run a compact station without turning a walking or
ferry-based activation into a battery-hauling exercise, while the spares make
multi-stop days less dependent on mid-trip charging.

One battery powered the complete [Flight of the Bumblebees operation from Block Island](/notes/2026-07-26-flight-of-the-bumblebees-from-block-island/)
and has also supported multi-stop KX3 outings.
