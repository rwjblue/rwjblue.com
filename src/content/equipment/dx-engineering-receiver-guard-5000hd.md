The Receiver Guard sits inline between the
[loop-on-ground receive antenna](/radio/equipment/loop-on-ground-receive-antenna/)
and the [Elecraft K4D](/radio/equipment/elecraft-k4d/). It adds protection at
the receiver input from strong RF produced when the station transmits on a
nearby antenna.

I bought this unit used through QRZ for roughly $40 to $50 less than the price
of a new one, then installed it the day it arrived.
