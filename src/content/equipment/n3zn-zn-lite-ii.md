The model is the ZN-Lite II, sometimes shortened in field notes and memory to
"QRP-Lite II." It is the lighter portable counterpart to the
[ZN-9ZX](/radio/equipment/n3zn-zn-9zx/) and works well on a magnetic leg plate
when there is no stable table.

It was the CW paddle for the
[East Bay three-park rove](/notes/2026-05-30-east-bay-pota-rove/)
and the first day of the
[Rhode Island-to-Florida rove](/notes/2026-06-19-rhode-island-to-florida-rove-day-one/).
