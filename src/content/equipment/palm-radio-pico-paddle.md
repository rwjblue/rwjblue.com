The discontinued Pico is small enough to disappear into a field kit, with
finger pieces that retract into the housing for transport. Its size is the
appeal; the tradeoff is that the operating position and cable need to be kept
under control.

I used it during the
[Memorial Day Rhode Island rove](/notes/2026-05-25-pota-rove/), where an
intermittent stuck dit forced a mid-activation switch to the Zippy paddle.
