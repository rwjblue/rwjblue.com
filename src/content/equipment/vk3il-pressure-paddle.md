The VK3IL v2 design senses pressure instead of moving a conventional lever.
This pre-built version came from VE6LK and is light enough for walking
activations where a weighted desk paddle would be disproportionate.

It has been used with both the KX2 and KX3, including the
[Black Hut hike-in activation](/notes/2026-05-27-black-hut-wildlife-management-area-pota/)
and the early walking stops of the
[Washington, DC rove](/notes/2026-06-21-washington-dc-pota-rove/).
