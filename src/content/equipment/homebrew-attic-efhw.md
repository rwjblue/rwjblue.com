This is a homebrew end-fed half-wave installed in a horseshoe shape around the
perimeter of the attic. It uses a TennTennas 49:1 transformer and a short
counterpoise that is roughly one to two meters long.

![Inside the TennTennas 49-to-1 transformer enclosure, showing the toroidal core and winding](/images/equipment/homebrew-attic-efhw/tenntennas-49-to-1-interior.jpg)

There is a feedpoint choke, but the coax is loosely wrapped around a Mix 43
core. That was built with the parts and understanding I had at the time. I may
rewind it on Mix 31 or another more appropriate core when I revisit the
installation.

The antenna is one of the HF paths connected to the Elecraft K4D.
