The throw line and bag turn available trees into antenna supports without
making every wire antenna carry its own launch method. The kit belongs with
masts and supports because it moves between Spooltenna, PackTenna, Reliance,
and other field wires.

Successful throws materially changed the setup at the
[Black Hut hike-in activation](/notes/2026-05-27-black-hut-wildlife-management-area-pota/),
where the far end of the Spooltenna wire reached roughly 35 to 40 feet.
