The Z-11ProII provides the matching stage in the ready FT-891 and Scout-XF
bag. It is especially useful when that station uses the PackTenna random wire
or another antenna that is not naturally resonant on the operating band.

LDG rates it for 1.8 through 54 MHz and up to 125 watts SSB or 100 watts CW.
It is also available from
[GigaParts](https://www.gigaparts.com/ldg-electronics-z-11proii.html).
