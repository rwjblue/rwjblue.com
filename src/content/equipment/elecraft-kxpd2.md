The KXPD2 mounts to the front of either KX-series portable radio, eliminating a
separate cable and heavy base. That makes it especially useful for standing,
handheld, or lap-balanced operating positions.

It had its first field outing with my KX2 at
[Durfee Hill](/notes/2026-05-29-durfee-hill-wildlife-management-area-pota/)
and was the compact fallback for the difficult
[Lincoln Memorial activation](/notes/2026-06-21-washington-dc-pota-rove/).
