This is the prebuilt ZM-2 with BNC connectors. It is a manual Z-match tuner
with two variable-capacitor controls, built-in support for coax-fed and
balanced-line antennas, and no battery or control-cable requirement.

Emtech rates it for 80 through 10 meters, 15 watts maximum, and a weight of
8 ounces. It serves as a compact field tuner for experimental wire antennas
and as a fallback when an automatic tuner is unavailable or cannot find a
match.
