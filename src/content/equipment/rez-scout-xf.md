This entry intentionally treats the Scout-XF as one working system rather than
cataloging every spike, whip, coil, radial, strap, and adapter separately. The
parts are useful because they combine into several quick field configurations.

The current module set includes:

- 25-foot and 17-foot telescoping whips
- a 40-meter loading coil for each whip length
- the X-Form 4:1 transformer used for Rybakov-style configurations

The system has supported compact beach stations, including the [Flight of the Bumblebees operation from Block Island](/notes/2026-07-26-flight-of-the-bumblebees-from-block-island/),
where the 25-foot whip and 4:1 transformer made a portable multiband vertical.

The individual modules are available from DX Engineering as the
[25-foot 40-meter coil](https://www.dxengineering.com/parts/rez-sc-4025),
[17-foot 40-meter coil](https://www.dxengineering.com/parts/rez-sc-4017), and
[X-Form 4:1](https://www.dxengineering.com/parts/rez-sxf-41).
