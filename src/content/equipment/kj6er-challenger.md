The Challenger is a repeatable antenna configuration rather than a single
factory product. Mine uses a 25-foot telescoping whip for the long side, a
linked wire for the short side, and a 4:1 transformer. It can stand on a tripod
where stakes and tree-supported wires are impractical.

[Greg Mihran, KJ6ER's design document](https://www.vhfclub.org/pdf/Challenger%20Antenna%20by%20KJ6ER%20%282025-02%29.pdf)
describes the 20-through-6-meter off-center-fed half-wave system. It became one
of my most frequently used portable antennas during the
[Memorial Day Rhode Island rove](/notes/2026-05-25-pota-rove/) and the
[Washington DC rove](/notes/2026-06-21-washington-dc-pota-rove/).
