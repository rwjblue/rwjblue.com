The Challenger is Greg Mihran, KJ6ER's 20-through-6-meter off-center-fed
half-wave vertical design. Mine is the
[complete system assembled by J-Systems](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4384775782/challenger-antenna-system),
with a 25-foot telescoping whip for the long side, a linked wire for the short
side, and a 4:1 transformer. J-Systems did an excellent job building it. The
antenna can stand on a tripod where stakes and tree-supported wires are
impractical.

[KJ6ER's antenna resource page](https://www.k6ldf.com/greg-mihran-kj6er/)
includes the antenna primer and the complete
[Challenger plans and specifications](https://bit.ly/KJ6ERChallenger).
The design is fully documented for anyone who wants to build one; I bought the
assembled version because I did not have time to make it myself.

The Challenger became one of my most frequently used portable antennas during
the [Memorial Day Rhode Island rove](/notes/2026-05-25-pota-rove/) and the
[Washington DC rove](/notes/2026-06-21-washington-dc-pota-rove/).
