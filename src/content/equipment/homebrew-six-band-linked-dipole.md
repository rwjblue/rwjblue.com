The target is a resonant linked dipole covering 15, 17, 20, 30, 40, and 80
meters. Opening and closing links between wire sections will select the active
length for each band without requiring a tuner to make a non-resonant element
usable.

The first step is a simpler single-band build around a
[3D-printed center insulator](https://www.printables.com/model/1750025-dipole-center-insulator).
That prototype can establish the feedpoint construction, strain relief, wire
attachment, and deployment method before the additional linked sections are
added. The finished build should record the as-built segment lengths and link
configuration for every band.
