The immediate plan is a basic single-band dipole built around a
[3D-printed center insulator](https://www.printables.com/model/1750025-dipole-center-insulator).
That first build can establish the feedpoint construction, strain relief, wire
attachment, tuning process, and deployment method without committing the final
antenna to this particular center. A more portable version may use a smaller
center that packs with, or directly onto, a wire winder.

The longer-term idea is a resonant linked dipole covering 15, 17, 20, 30, 40,
and 80 meters, but its physical layout is still open. One possibility is to
keep the shorter linked sections permanently together on a single winder, then
attach separate wire extensions for the longer bands. Testing will determine
whether that split belongs before 40 meters or only before 80 meters, and
whether the complete six-band arrangement remains practical enough to carry.
