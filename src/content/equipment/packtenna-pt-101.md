This wire is one of the fastest ways to put a multiband station on the air. It
packs small, can hang from a portable mast or available support, and works well
with radios that provide an appropriate tuner.

The combination of this wire, a counterpoise, and the FT-891 became a repeatable
car-adjacent setup during the [Rhode Island-to-Florida rove](/notes/2026-06-22-rhode-island-to-florida-rove-day-four/).
