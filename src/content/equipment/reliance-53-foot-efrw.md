This is the longer random-wire option in the field inventory. I commonly think
of it as roughly 51 feet, but Reliance's current product name and specification
identify the supplied element as 53 feet, so the inventory uses that exact
length.

The 9:1 transformer and tuner-assisted design trade resonance for broad
coverage and flexible installation. It is most useful when there is room for a
longer wire and a tuner is already part of the station.
