The Epic PWRgate is the handoff point between normal shack power and the
[280 Ah LiTime battery](/radio/equipment/litime-280ah/). It allows the station
loads to continue from the battery when the power supply is unavailable and
provides a controlled charging path instead of placing the supply directly
across the battery.

This page covers the power-management device itself; distribution blocks,
Powerpole jumpers, fuses, and ordinary cables remain supporting infrastructure.
