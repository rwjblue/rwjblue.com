The FT-100D was my original HF radio when I first earned General in 2006. I did
not spend much time on HF before that license expired in 2007, but my wife kept
the radio. Finding it again helped start the chain of events that brought me
back to amateur radio.

It is currently stored and not part of the active station. I am keeping it
because it is a capable compact all-band radio, because I still like it, and
because its history matters more than its present utilization.
