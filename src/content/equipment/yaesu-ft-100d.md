The FT-100D was my original HF radio after I earned General in 1999. I did not
spend much time on HF before radio gave way to work and a growing family. When
my license later expired, my wife kept the radio and the rest of the station
carefully packed away. Finding it again helped start the chain of events that
brought me back to amateur radio.

It is currently stored and not part of the active station. I am keeping it
because it is a capable compact all-band radio, because I still like it, and
because its history matters more than its present utilization.
