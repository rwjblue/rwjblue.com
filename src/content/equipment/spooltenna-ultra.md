The two Ultras are compact 40-through-10-meter end-fed half-wave antennas built
for fast field deployment. The spool form keeps the transformer and antenna
wire together and makes teardown much less likely to end in a tangle.

I also have the [PARKS 80-meter accessory wire](https://www.spooltenna.com/products/parks-80m-antenna-wire-accessory).
Its separate SpareSpool and plug-in wire let either Ultra transformer support a
full-length 80-through-10-meter setup without making the accessory look like a
third complete antenna.

That combination was used for the [K2HRC Rhode Island POTA activation](/notes/2026-07-18-k2hrc-rhode-island-pota/).
The standard Ultra has also been used for compact hike-in and picnic-table
stations.
