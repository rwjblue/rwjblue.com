The Micro PA50-3 is a small external HF amplifier for QRP radios. Mine is the
third-generation model, which arrived on September 11, 2026. I bought it because
it is small enough to fit easily into my backpacking setup.

With the [Elecraft KX2](/radio/equipment/elecraft-kx2/), I was getting 50 watts
out with about 3.6 watts of drive.

The amplifier needs a well-matched antenna system: a resonant antenna with a
good 50-ohm match, or an external tuner between the amplifier and antenna,
rated for the amplifier's output power. The KX2's internal tuner cannot match
the antenna through the amplifier.

HamGeek rates it for 3.5 through 30 MHz, with nominal 50-watt output at 13.8 V
from 1 to 5 watts of drive. It includes automatically switched low-pass filters,
RF-triggered transmit/receive switching, and an OLED display for power, SWR,
supply voltage, and temperature. The amplifier weighs about 0.65 kg and accepts
a 10 to 17.5 V supply, with lower supply voltage reducing output power.
