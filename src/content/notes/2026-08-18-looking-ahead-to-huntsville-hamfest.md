The [2026 Huntsville Hamfest](https://hamfest.org/) is also this year's ARRL
National Convention, with Four Days In August activities leading into the main
hamfest weekend. There is more happening than I can reasonably follow, which
makes choosing what to miss almost as important as choosing what to attend.

[Spencer Callicott, KQ4VYY](https://www.qrz.com/db/KQ4VYY), built an excellent
unofficial [searchable Hamfest Guide](https://hamfest.guide/). It combines the
hamfest, convention, and Four Days In August schedules, lets me star a personal
agenda, makes conflicts visible, and remains available offline. I will use that
for the complete timeline rather than reproduce it here.

This note is the smaller list: the questions and themes I want to carry with me
through the weekend.

## Carry the CW conversation forward

Huntsville comes less than a week after my
[**Morse Code for the Technician** session](/notes/2026-07-31-morse-code-for-the-technician-resources/)
at Northeast HamXposition. I want to pay attention to the questions people ask
after that talk, especially the places where a list of learning resources is
not enough.

Those questions may point toward better practice tools, clearer first-contact
guidance, or a more useful way to help someone move from recognizing characters
to making an ordinary contact. I also want to listen for ideas that help me
continue my own journey: moving beyond predictable exchanges, improving head
copy, and becoming more comfortable when a CW contact turns into a conversation.

## Spend the weekend with people

The real priority is spending time with other hams. I hope to catch up with
friends I know from Discord, people I met at HamCation 2026, and some of the
POTA operators I have come to know through activations and online communities.

I hope there will be a POTA gathering area or another natural place for that
group to collect. A schedule can help me find the structured parts of the
weekend, but those conversations are the part I most want to protect.

## Keep the portable-antenna workshop as Friday's plan

I registered for Friday's day-long
[Salty Walt's Portable Antenna Building Workshop](https://hamfest.guide/) as
part of the ARRL National Convention. It is led by
[Walt Hudson, K4OGO](https://www.qrz.com/db/K4OGO) and lines up directly with
the portable-antenna experiments behind many of my POTA field notes.

I may not spend the entire day there. If friends are making plans together, I
will probably choose the time with them. If I am not hanging out with other
people, though, the workshop is exactly where I want to be.

## Pack for a week and a half

HamXposition, Huntsville, and the following work trip to San Francisco turn
into roughly a week and a half of back-to-back travel. I hope to bring a small
POTA station to California, but every radio item has to earn its space alongside
everything else I need for the trip.

That makes the size of the station a real decision rather than an ordinary
packing exercise. It also puts a limit on the hamfest haul: anything I buy has
to fit in the luggage I already have, or be worth the trouble and cost of
shipping it home.

## Get into the Brass Knuckle Gang

The one absolute requirement for the weekend is getting into BKG, the Brass
Knuckle Gang. It is a deliberately informal two-meter CW "club" originally
organized by [Forrest, KI7QCF](https://www.qrz.com/db/KI7QCF), through his
[Utah Forest YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCrNlTPz0S19qL3GiHqV8Yug).

I still need to confirm where the group will gather and what I need to do, but
leaving Huntsville without joining would feel like missing the one fixed goal
on an otherwise flexible agenda.

## Decisions to make before publishing

- Which friends and POTA groups are making plans for Friday and the weekend?
- Is there a POTA gathering area or scheduled meetup?
- Where and when is the Brass Knuckle Gang gathering, and what does joining
  require?
- How much of Friday will I spend in the portable-antenna workshop?
- What is the smallest useful San Francisco POTA station, and how will it fit
  into the luggage for the full trip?
- What is the threshold for carrying a hamfest purchase versus shipping it
  home?
- What single question would make the eventual Huntsville recap useful rather
  than comprehensive?
