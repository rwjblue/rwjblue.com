On Sunday, September 13, I am volunteering with the Westerly Amateur Radio
Team to support the
[2026 Surftown Half Marathon, Relay & 5K](https://www.hartfordmarathon.com/surftown-half-marathon-relay-5k/)
in Westerly, Rhode Island. The races start and finish beside Misquamicut Beach,
with the half-marathon course continuing through Watch Hill and Weekapaug.

[Jim Creamer, KB1MAO](https://www.qrz.com/db/KB1MAO), put out the call for
operators. I was happy to sign up, and I also offered to share the request at a
Thursday meeting of the Fidelity Amateur Radio Club. A course this large needs
quite a few people positioned along the route, including non-hams who can work
alongside the radio operators.

My exact assignment is still to come, but the radio plan and race-day schedule
already give me a useful starting point.

## The communications plan

The primary net will use the **147.315 MHz repeater**, with a positive 600 kHz
offset and a 110.9 Hz CTCSS tone. **146.550 MHz simplex** is the secondary
frequency. Both need to be programmed and tested before race morning.

The equipment guidance is practical for a long morning spread across the
course: a mobile radio or a good handheld with an effective antenna, spare
batteries, a cell phone, an ICS 214 activity log, the course materials, a
notebook, and clothing suitable for the weather. A safety vest is required for
anyone working on or near the road.

The communication rules also keep the radio traffic focused. Safety is the
first concern. An injury or illness that needs emergency help goes to 911 first
and net control second. Reports over the air should use a runner's bib number,
not their name. Net control can then coordinate with the event staff and public
safety.

## Race-morning plan

Net-control setup begins at 5:30 AM in the Misquamicut Business Association
parking lot. The whole team briefs there at 6:30. Atlantic Avenue and other
course roads close to vehicles at 7:00, so arriving early is not optional for
operators who need to reach their assignments.

The runners line up at 7:15. The half marathon starts at 7:30 and initially
heads west toward Watch Hill. After returning through Misquamicut, the course
continues east toward Weekapaug before coming back to the finish. The 5K heads
east from Misquamicut and returns along the beach community.

The schedule has the race ending at approximately 10:00 and the communications
operation packed up by about 11:00. The actual release time will depend on the
assignment and the final runners clearing that portion of the course.
