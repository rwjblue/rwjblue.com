This is the companion page for my **Morse Code for the Technician** session in
the 2026 Northeast HamXposition Technician Toolkit. The central idea is simple:
learn characters as sounds, build a short practice habit, and make the first
contact small and safe.

## Choose a learning path

- [Long Island CW Club](https://longislandcwclub.org/) offers ongoing live
  classes at multiple levels.
- [CWops CW Academy](https://cwa.cwops.org/) offers coached, session-based
  classes from beginner through advanced levels.
- [CW Innovations](https://cwinnovations.net/) focuses on conversational CW,
  head copy, and confidence on the air.

You do not need all three. Pick the structure and schedule you are most likely
to keep using.

## Practice that fits your day

- [Morse Mania for iPhone and iPad](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/morse-mania-learn-morse-code/id1511042196)
  and [Morse Mania for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=net.countrymania.morse)
  provide focused, interactive character practice.
- [Morse Code Ninja](https://www.morsecode.ninja/learn/) provides incremental
  audio, podcast, and video practice. Its audio lessons fit especially well
  into a commute or walk.
- [Vail Morse](https://vailmorse.com/) combines a browser decoder with an
  internet repeater. The optional [Vail Adapter](https://vailadapter.com/)
  connects a physical key or paddle by USB.
- [My CW practice tools](/radio/cw-practice/) include POTA exchanges and other
  short operating patterns.

Start with one sound-based tool. Use a normal character speed with extra space
between characters, rather than slowing each character until you can count its
parts.

## Get on the air without making the first contact enormous

- [Parks on the Air](https://parksontheair.com/) gives a new operator a short,
  repeated, and predictable exchange to listen for.
- [My first all-CW POTA activation](https://qrper.com/2026/04/n1rwj-my-first-cw-pota-activation-dont-wait-to-try-it/)
  is the field report behind the “do not wait” story in the talk.
- [The K8CES Zippy Paddle guide](/notes/2026-07-29-building-k8ces-zippy-paddle/)
  documents the small paddle I am building for the HamXposition session.

Hunt before you activate. Listen through a few contacts, recognize your own
call, learn `AGN?` and `QRS PSE`, and arrange a patient first QSO if that makes
the step feel safer. POTA can be the gateway; it does not have to be the
destination.
